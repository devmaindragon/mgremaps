FAQ

My car is not listed. Email the make, model, year and engine code and we will add it in the next update.

How exact are the figures? Peak values are estimated gains for healthy vehicles. The curves are representative drawings derived from those peaks, not dyno output.

A dealer number is wrong. Write to us and we will correct the list.

How do I get on the leaderboard? Send your dyno result to the dealer who did the tune; entries come through them.

The app opens in the wrong language. Use the language button at the bottom; your choice is remembered.
